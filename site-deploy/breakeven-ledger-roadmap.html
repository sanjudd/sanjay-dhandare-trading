// Why breakeven happens
Breakeven is a discipline problem, not a strategy problem.
After a year in F&O, most traders who are flat have a working edge somewhere in their entries. What eats the edge is what happens around the trade — size, frequency, and exits. This roadmap is sequenced to fix that order first.

The leak

Overtrading: taking a 4th or 5th "setup" after the plan for the day is done, usually to recover a loss or boredom-trade a flat market.

Weak entry/exit: entering before confirmation, then exiting winners early on fear and losers late on hope — which quietly inverts your risk:reward.

The fix in this roadmap

Hard trade cap: 3 trades/day is a circuit breaker, enforced in Phase 2 before any strategy work continues.

Rule-based exits: Phase 5 replaces feel-based exits with a written checklist, so 1:2 becomes the floor, not the exception.

The roadmap: fundamentals → advanced
Each stage has a graduation criterion. You don't move on because a month has passed — you move on because your trade journal proves the rule is now automatic.
01
Foundation Audit
Weeks 1–2
You've traded a year, so this isn't "learn what F&O is" — it's a hard reset on mechanics you may have half-learned from screens and forums: contract specs, margin (SPAN + exposure), settlement, and exactly how your broker's order types behave under fast moves.
Cover
  • Futures vs options mechanics, lot sizes, expiry cycles
  • SPAN + exposure margin, and margin calls on your broker
  • Order types: SL-M vs SL-L, and slippage in fast markets
  • Audit your last 100 trades: tag each as rule-based or impulse
Resources
  • Zerodha Varsity — Futures & Options modules
  • NSE India — official F&O contract specs
  • Video: NSE F&O margin explained
Tools
  • Your broker's contract note + margin calculator
  • A spreadsheet or journal to log all trades from Phase 2 onward
Graduate when: you can state your exact margin requirement and worst-case loss for any position before placing it, without checking a calculator mid-trade.
02
Risk Management Bedrock
Weeks 3–5
This is the phase that actually fixes breakeven. Before any new strategy work, position sizing and the daily trade cap get installed as hard rules, not guidelines.
Cover
  • Position sizing from stop-loss distance, not from "feel"
  • Fixed fractional risk: 1–2% of ₹1,00,000 per trade
  • Daily loss limit (2 stop-outs = done) and weekly drawdown cap
  • The 3-trades/day rule as a circuit breaker, not a target
Resources
  • Zerodha Varsity — Position sizing
  • Investopedia — Risk management essentials
  • Video: position sizing for options traders
Tools
  • Use the Edge Calculator above with your real risk% and R:R
  • Pre-trade checklist template (size, stop, target, R:R written before entry)
Graduate when: 20 straight trading days with zero trades exceeding 2% risk, and zero days above 3 trades.
03
Trading Psychology & Overtrading Control
Weeks 6–8
The rules from Phase 2 will break under emotional pressure unless you can name the pressure. This phase builds the journal habit and the vocabulary to catch revenge trading and FOMO before the order goes in.
Cover
  • Revenge trading, FOMO entries, and boredom trades — spot the pattern in your own log
  • Pre-market routine and a hard "no-trade" list (news days, low conviction)
  • Daily journal: entry reason, emotion at entry, emotion at exit
Resources
  • Zerodha Varsity — Trading psychology
  • Video: why traders overtrade
  • Video: how to keep a trading journal
Tools
  • A journal template with an emotion column, not just P&L
  • Weekly review: count impulse trades vs planned trades
Graduate when: a full month's journal shows every trade matched a written setup — no unplanned entries.
04
Strategy & Edge Development
Weeks 9–14
Now — and only now — refine the actual entries. Pick one or two setups you can define in one sentence, and prove them on paper before real size.
Cover
  • Price action: support/resistance, trend structure, volume confirmation
  • Pick ONE primary setup (e.g. breakout-retest or trend pullback) and define entry, stop, target rules precisely
  • Backtest 100+ historical instances of that setup by hand
Resources
  • Zerodha Varsity — Technical analysis module
  • TradingView — charting & replay for backtesting
  • Video: price action strategy for Nifty/BankNifty
Tools
  • TradingView bar replay for manual backtesting
  • A one-page "setup sheet": conditions, entry trigger, invalidation
Graduate when: your hand-backtest shows a positive expectancy at 1:2+ R:R across at least 100 samples.
05
Execution Discipline
Weeks 15–18
This is where "deep things like entry/exit" get solved directly — by removing discretion from the exit and replacing it with a checklist executed at small size.
Cover
  • Entry checklist: trigger candle, confirmation, no chasing extended moves
  • Exit rules fixed BEFORE entry — stop and target as actual GTT/SL orders, not mental levels
  • Partial booking rules if used (e.g. book half at 1:1, trail rest to 1:2+)
  • Live trading resumes at 25–30% of normal size while rules bed in
Resources
  • Zerodha Varsity — Stop-loss placement
  • Video: building an entry/exit checklist
Tools
  • Bracket / GTT orders so stop and target are placed with the entry, not after
  • Printed checklist taped to your monitor for the first 3 weeks
Graduate when: 4 straight weeks with every exit matching the pre-set stop or target — no manual override.
06
Scaling to Consistency
Weeks 19–26
With rules holding under real trades, scale size gradually and start tracking the metrics that actually predict the 3–5% monthly target: win rate, average R:R, and expectancy.
Cover
  • Scale from 30% → 60% → 100% of target size, one step per profitable fortnight
  • Track win rate and realised R:R weekly against the Edge Calculator assumptions
  • Build a simple monthly P&L statement like a small business, not a gamble
Resources
  • Zerodha Varsity — Trading expectancy
  • Video: expectancy, win rate and R:R explained
Tools
  • Monthly scorecard: trades, win rate, avg R:R, expectancy, drawdown
Graduate when: 3 consecutive profitable months landing inside the 3–5% band without breaching the daily loss limit.
07
Advanced Mastery — Options Greeks & Structures
Month 7–8
Only add complexity once single-leg directional trading is consistently profitable. This phase layers in Greeks-aware trading and defined-risk multi-leg structures.
Cover
  • Delta, theta, vega, gamma — how each moves your P&L intraday and near expiry
  • IV rank/percentile and why buying options in high IV hurts your edge
  • Defined-risk spreads: bull/bear spreads, iron condors for range days
  • Hedging a directional bet with a cheap opposite-side option
Resources
  • Zerodha Varsity — Option Theory module
  • Sensibull — options strategy builder & payoff analysis
  • Opstra — options analytics & backtesting
  • Video: option Greeks explained
Tools
  • Options chain with live Greeks (Sensibull / broker terminal)
  • Payoff diagram tool before entering any multi-leg structure
Graduate when: you can explain, before entry, exactly how theta and IV will affect a spread's P&L by expiry — not just the direction bet.
08
Running It as a Business
Month 9+
Profitability without systemisation decays. The last stage turns the roadmap into a repeating cycle: review, refine, re-certify against your own rules.
Cover
  • Quarterly strategy review: which setups are actually earning their keep
  • Capital allocation as account grows — when to raise risk% vs add capital
  • F&O taxation basics (business income, turnover, audit thresholds) and record-keeping
  • A written "trading business plan" reviewed every quarter
Resources
  • Zerodha Varsity — Taxation for traders
  • SEBI Investor Education
  • Video: F&O taxation and turnover rules
Tools
  • Quarterly scorecard review against Phase 6 metrics
  • A CA familiar with F&O turnover once volumes grow
Graduate when: the system runs without you needing willpower to follow it — it's just how you trade.

21 Techniques to Become a Profitable Trader

  1. Acquire knowledge from a trusted source
  2. In initial times, expect only 50% accuracy
  3. Take a minimum profit of 1:2 (risk to reward ratio)
  4. Create rules (SOP)
  5. Always use verified and tested strategies
  6. Back-test your strategy
  7. Practice your system 100 times, so you can trust it
  8. Trade only your system, not emotion — trade like a robot
  9. We can understand only 10% of the market
  10. Master risk management with only 2.5% risk per trade
  11. Never blame the market — take responsibility for your decisions
  12. Cut losses quickly and let profits run
  13. Keep a trading journal
  14. Avoid full-capacity trades
  15. Join a trading community
  16. 1 to 3 trades daily is the best strategy for success
  17. Focus on one strategy at a time
  18. Stay disciplined, stay consistent & patient
  19. Avoid FOMO (fear of missing out)
  20. Trade only in high-probability setups
  21. Learn from other's mistakes

Change Your Words, Change Your Life

"Don't speak negatively about yourself, even as a joke. Your body doesn't know the difference." — Bruce Lee

"Trading is too hard for me."Trading is a skill I can develop with practice and patience.
"I can't handle market volatility."Volatility creates opportunities for smart investors.

"The market is against me."I adapt to market conditions and find opportunities.
