The refinement

One trigger. Two mirrored setups.

Same two lines, same one candle rule, read in opposite directions. Neither side fires without both conditions — the cross gives the direction, the candle gives the timing.

SHORT — SELL

9 EMA crosses under the 33 EMA

  1. 1The 9 EMA crosses below the 33 EMA — momentum has flipped down.
  2. 2A shooting star prints at that same crossover — the rally got rejected right there.
  3. 3There's clean room down to the next support — enough for 1:2 or better.
  4. 4Stop above the shooting star's high. Target the support. Sell.
LONG — BUY

9 EMA crosses over the 33 EMA

  1. 1The 9 EMA crosses above the 33 EMA — momentum has flipped up.
  2. 2A hammer prints at that same crossover — the sell-off got rejected right there.
  3. 3There's clean room up to the next resistance — enough for 1:2 or better.
  4. 4Stop below the hammer's low. Target the resistance. Buy.
