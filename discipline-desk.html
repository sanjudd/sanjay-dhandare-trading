    The three pillars

    Risk first

    • Risk 1% to 2% of capital on a trade.
    • Never move a stop further away.
    • Set the daily loss limit before opening charts.

    Process over profit

    • Judge the day by how well the plan was followed.
    • Treat losses as a normal business cost.
    • No revenge trade after a red trade.

    Routine and prep

    • Review macro data and key levels before the open.
    • Write the reason for every entry and exit.
    • Shut the terminal once the trade count is reached.
